# The World Today — Thursday 19 November 2026

A newspaper front page that sums up Thursday 19 November 2026 in a single
image. The lead story is the first-ever trilateral meeting of Donald Trump,
Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin in Shenzhen — it meets today, with nuclear
arms control and the war in Iran on the agenda, Ukraine off it, and no deal
expected. Below it: the US–Iran war widening to Kuwait and Bahrain, the
International Court of Justice reading its historic Rohingya genocide
verdict today, Anthropic's record $81.31 billion IPO debuting on the
Nasdaq, Gaza's fragile truce under strain, and a strip of the rest of the
day — Bangladesh's lockdown, the Kerch Bridge collapse, GTA VI's global
launch, a record $190.4 million Van Gogh, Cyclone Akvan, Ethiopia–Eritrea,
and Waymo's freeway ban. The image is drawn fresh in this repository by a
deterministic Pillow script (`dev/make_image.py` in the build record) from
news verified against Reuters, AP, BBC and CNN; nothing in it asserts an
outcome for the two scheduled events whose results were not yet known when
it was drawn.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

This copies `entry.png` to `out/entry.png` after checking it is a PNG of
at most 1 MiB and at most 1024×1024 pixels.
